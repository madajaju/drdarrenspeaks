## Navigation Inventory

Current primary nav was AAOS-heavy and repeated across pages:
`Home`, `AAOS`, `Keynotes`, `Workshops`, `About`, `Books`, `Podcasts`, `Resources`, `Media Kit`, `Book Dr. Darren`.

Current footer nav repeated many of the same links and also pushed users back into AAOS, podcasts, and resource pages.

Repeated links were the main problem, not broken markup. The old nav sent too much traffic to content hubs instead of the booking path.

Current clean target:
`Home`, `Speaking`, `Workshops`, `Books`, `Media Kit`, `About`, `Contact`.

Links to de-emphasize:
`AAOS`, `Podcasts`, `Resources`, and duplicate legacy bio routes.

Cross-property routing:
`aiaugmented.ai` for methodology and certification.
`paidar.ai` for consulting, implementation, and workshops as paid services.
`embracingdigital.org` for podcasts, articles, and research.
`drdarrenspeaks.com` for speaking, books, media kit, and booking.

`AAOS speaker`, `AAOS keynotes`, `technology adoption`, `future of work`, `podcasts`, and `resources` as lead positioning.

`AI-Augmented Leadership`, `AI-Augmented Teams`, `AI-Augmented Organizations`, `Reliable AI Execution`, `Human Judgment at Machine Speed`, `Trusted AI Adoption`, and `Enterprise AI Transformation`.
