Workshop

Educating the AI-Augmented

This workshop is for professors and teachers who want to help students use AI well without lowering standards. It focuses on course design, classroom practice, assessment, and the habits students need to think clearly in an AI-rich environment.

Book Dr. DarrenDiscuss a workshop

Audience

Professors, teachers, faculty leaders, and instructional teams.

Outcomes

  • Update teaching practices for AI-aware classrooms.
  • Set clear expectations for student AI use.
  • Redesign assignments and assessments for stronger evidence of learning.
  • Help students build judgment, ethics, and responsible AI habits.

Duration

  • 45 minutes
  • 60 minutes
  • 90 minutes
  • Half-day

Extension

Use this format when you want practical guidance for teaching in a way that keeps students engaged and accountable.
