Workshop

AI-Augmented Education

This workshop is for administrators and staff in education who need a practical path for adopting AI across the institution. It focuses on policy, governance, operations, staff readiness, and the change management needed to make adoption useful and sustainable.

Book Dr. DarrenDiscuss a workshop

Audience

Administrators, staff, deans, department leaders, student-success teams, and institutional decision makers.

Outcomes

  • Set workable policy and governance expectations.
  • Align AI use with institutional goals and student needs.
  • Improve staff workflows and service delivery.
  • Create a realistic adoption roadmap for the institution.

Duration

  • 45 minutes
  • 60 minutes
  • 90 minutes
  • Half-day

Extension

Use this format when the institution needs a clear operating path for AI, not just another strategy discussion.
